Our Story

Leveling Up Since 2017

Game Night Arcade opened in Coral Springs on August 25th, 2017. Since then we have grown and changed over the years, always striving to improve the arcade experience for our customers.

What started with a limited food menu and beer options has expanded to a full-service restaurant with craft beer and top-shelf liquor. We have also been blessed with the opportunity to double in size coming out of Covid at the end of 2021.

We are proud to be part of the local Coral Springs community, a strong family-oriented city in South Florida.
