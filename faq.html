FAQ & Info

Everything you need to know before you press start.

How we operate is SIMPLE: everyone makes one purchase (food or drink), and all the games are completely free to play! No quarters, tokens, or game cards required. Just grab a drink, find an open machine, and start playing.
If you are over 21 you purchase either food or drinks, alcohol is not a required purchase.
If you are under 21 you pay a $12 cover charge and that comes with your choice of chips and soda.
Game Night Arcade is a family-friendly environment during the day. However, because we feature a full-service bar with craft beer and top-shelf liquor, our venue shifts to a mature atmosphere later in the evening. Please contact us for specific age-restriction hours for tonight's events.
People under 18 are allowed in from 3pm to 10pm. After 10pm we are 18 and up.
We provide all the hardware! If a console station is open, simply ask one of our staff members or bartenders for a controller. We'll set you up with the game of your choice so you can get right into the action.
Yes. To ensure a premium experience and support our dedicated hardworking staff, a 20% service charge is automatically included on all checks. You still have the option to leave an additional tip for exceptional service, but it is not required.
